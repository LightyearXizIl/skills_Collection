# 武周场景指南

## 登基与朝会

### 登基正殿全景

- Grand wide or wide establishing shot, strict central axis and near-symmetry.
- Wu Zetian stands one step from or directly before the throne; the throne is immediately behind her.
- Two orderly rows of ministers in full official robes face her across a broad ceremonial aisle.
- The hall is prosperous, layered, and monumental; use columns, curtains, lanterns, bronze vessels, and carved architectural detail without clutter.
- Do not place an unexplained giant bell in front of the hall.

### 登基中景

- Keep the throne close behind her and retain enough black-gold robe to communicate status.
- Ministers may be suggested only at the lower or lateral edges when required.

### 登基胸像特写

- Frame from chest to the top of the complete headdress.
- The throne is very close behind and softly readable.
- No ministers behind her; no smile; no coquettish movement.
- Lead with makeup, expression, headwear, and black-gold robe.

### 劝阻称帝

- The minister faces Wu Zetian and speaks with visible urgency and fear, not polite restraint.
- Wu Zetian remains one step from the throne; her stillness contrasts with the minister's agitation.
- Use a clear eyeline and spatial relationship rather than isolated talking-head portraits.

### 反打群臣

- Use an over-the-shoulder reverse shot from behind Wu Zetian toward kneeling or bowing ministers.
- Her headdress or shoulder edge anchors the foreground; the broad aisle and ordered ranks remain visible.

## 御案与政令

- For a hand-only insert, show only the hands, sleeves, brush, paper, inkstone, seal, and imperial desk; no face or torso.
- A traditional calligraphy brush is held vertically. Avoid modern pens and generated readable Chinese.
- Direct overhead bird's-eye view means the camera is truly above the desk, with the layout simple and legible.
- Use “abstract black calligraphic strokes, no readable text” when the exact character is not essential.

## 科举

- Wide or direct overhead examination hall.
- Adult male and female candidates sit at separate low wooden desks.
- Every visible writing hand uses a traditional brush vertically; include paper, inkstone, and brush rest.
- Examiners in official robes supervise or record; Wu Zetian is absent unless explicitly requested.
- Ordered repetition communicates institutional reform; avoid classroom modernity.

## 武举

- Show male and female candidates in the same examination system.
- Use riding archery, standing archery, weapon forms, straw targets, and robed examiners recording results.
- Dynamic dust and fabric are welcome, but the scene is an examination ground, not a battlefield.

## 薄赋轻徭

- A full-robed official in a black futou returns a sealed grain sack or cancels a tally before an elderly farmer and family.
- A clerk closes a tax register or collects unused labor tallies.
- Use a granary courtyard, weighing scale, baskets, carts, and restrained relief.
- Avoid modern currency, performative charity, excessive misery, and clothing the official like a commoner.

## 农桑水利

- Show flowing canals, shallow weirs, water gates, fertile fields, farmers, ox carts, granaries, mulberry or silk work as appropriate.
- Let infrastructure and ordinary life communicate policy; do not reduce the entire theme to people hoeing a field.

## 边关与丝路

- Fortified gate, watchtowers, lowered military weapons, caravans, camels, horses, cloth bales, and emissaries.
- Use organized passage and secure trade rather than combat unless war is explicitly requested.
- Avoid fantasy armor and generic Central Asian stereotypes.

## 女子群像

- Compose women of varied ages and roles around study, textiles, ceremony, commerce, or public life.
- Use a unified gaze or shared action when symbolic uplift is required.
- Do not make every woman identical in face, dress, age, or hairstyle.

## 官兵民跪拜

- Exterior palace forecourt in a grand wide shot.
- Civil officials occupy the central/front ranks; armored soldiers line both sides; common men and women fill the rear.
- All kneel or bow in disciplined symmetrical formations around a broad empty central avenue.
- No chaotic festival crowd, raised weapons, or modern military organization.

## 武周宫城与山河

- For “大好山河,” prioritize a palace aerial rather than generic wilderness.
- The imperial palace fills most of the image; city wards, river, fields, plains, and mountains recede in layers.
- Use sunrise or sunset only when requested; otherwise preserve clear neutral-warm daylight.
