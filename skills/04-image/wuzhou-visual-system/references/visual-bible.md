# 武周视觉圣经

## 1. 总体定位

The world is Wu Zhou-era Shendu Luoyang presented as realistic historical cinema: monumental but inhabited, dignified rather than fantastical, and rich without visual clutter.

Separate two systems:

- **Wu Zhou system:** historical period, life stage, makeup, costume, architecture, props, hierarchy, crowd behavior, and composition.
- **Oriental Ancient Style contribution:** transparent natural light, warm-cool balance, low-saturation Eastern colors, realistic skin, fabric, wood, stone, and gentle cinematic contrast. Apply this contribution only when explicitly invoked.

## 2. Shared visual anchors

### Palette

- Main: ink black, antique gold, warm ivory, muted vermilion, dusty blue-gray, aged bronze, dark wood.
- Keep saturation restrained. Gold reads as material reflection, not a yellow filter.
- Preserve cool shadows and warm light; avoid uniform orange grading.

### Light

- Clear, directional natural light with controlled highlights.
- Soft atmospheric depth, mild haze only when motivated by scale or incense.
- Natural glowing skin with visible texture; no plastic skin, synthetic bloom, or blown highlights.
- Interiors may mix window daylight and restrained candle or lantern warmth.

### Materials

- Silk, brocade, linen, dark lacquered wood, carved stone, aged bronze, paper, ink, leather, and lamellar armor must retain believable texture and weight.
- Garments move with gravity and wind; avoid weightless fantasy fabric.

### Cinematography

- Documentary historical-film realism, 35mm or 50mm lens by default.
- Use 85mm only for controlled portrait close-ups.
- Use foreground occlusion sparingly to create depth, never to hide required costume or headwear.

## 3. Wu Zetian identity system

### A. Coronation state

This is the user's deliberate beautified art direction, not a general historical-age rule.

- She appears as an elegant beautiful woman in her twenties, with only faint mature fine lines and three to five subtle silver-white strands.
- Never describe her as elderly, frail, girlish, coquettish, seductive, or smiling.
- Expression: solemn, majestic, calm, controlled, sovereign; direct or slightly elevated gaze.
- Pose: still, upright, economical; hands composed; no dance-like sleeves or alluring gestures.

#### Makeup — place early in the prompt

Use strong formal Tang imperial makeup:

- translucent pale-ivory base with realistic skin texture;
- long, slender, slightly raised dark brows;
- elongated black-brown eyeliner;
- warm-red eyelids;
- a small red forehead huadian;
- faint pale-red xiehong near the temples;
- deep vermilion lips.

#### Headdress

- Complete black-and-gold female imperial coronation headdress.
- Tall black base; central gilded phoenix or imperial ornament.
- Symmetrical gold side ornaments, pearl strands, and fine gold chains.
- The whole headdress must be visible and uncropped when the requested frame includes it.

#### Robe

- Heavy layered black-and-gold imperial coronation robe.
- Dense but controlled gold embroidery, broad sleeves, dark layered collar, long weighted hem.
- The robe must feel ceremonial and authoritative, never bridal, fantasy, or stage-costume-like.

### B. Young palace-era Wu Meiniang

- Tang court attire appropriate to her rank and age; refined but not imperial.
- Period hairstyle and restrained hair ornaments; no coronation crown.
- Alert, ambitious intelligence may be visible, but do not use a modern power-pose cliché.

### C. Ganye Temple period

- Buddhist nun identity must be unmistakable.
- Gray, blue-gray, or warm-white layered monastic robes; restrained linen texture.
- Simple head covering or historically plausible nun styling.
- Sparse temple cell, wooden desk, sutra, candle, or cold window light; no palace luxury and no elaborate jewelry.

### D. Empress and ruler in administration

- Use mature composure, formal dark robes, controlled makeup, and concise gestures.
- When shown reviewing policy, the setting is an imperial study or audience hall, not a modern office.

## 4. Other people

### Officials

- Full Tang/Wu Zhou formal official robes and black futou hats.
- Rank and ceremony determine color and ornament; avoid generic white-robed crowds unless explicitly directed.
- Orderly paired rows, tablets held correctly, restrained gestures.

### Soldiers

- Historically plausible lamellar armor, cloth underlayers, leather fittings, spears or bows.
- In ceremonial scenes, weapons remain lowered and formations stay disciplined.

### Common people

- Layered Tang clothing with class differences; practical fabrics and muted colors.
- Include men, women, elders, and children only when the scene requires them.
- Avoid modern haircuts, cosmetics, shoes, accessories, and crowd behavior.

### Women as a collective

- Use books, scrolls, textiles, examinations, craft, ceremony, or public life to express opportunity.
- Avoid turning every scene into three or four young women; cast size follows the story and image function.

## 5. Architecture and geography

### Shendu Luoyang and imperial palace

- Early Tang/Wu Zhou timber architecture: vermilion columns and walls, dark tiled roofs, bracket sets, deep eaves, massive stone terraces, broad stairs, axial courtyards, covered corridors.
- Scale is open, layered, and ceremonial. The palace complex feels prosperous and metropolitan.
- Avoid Ming-Qing Forbidden City styling, yellow glazed Qing roofs, Manchu costume, and modern structures.
- Do not place a giant bell, pagoda, statue, or ceremonial prop at the palace front unless requested by the scene.

### Palace aerial

- The palace complex occupies most of the frame.
- Use an oblique bird's-eye view aligned to the central ceremonial axis.
- Gates, terraces, courtyards, and great halls layer toward the horizon; city wards extend beyond.
- Luo River, plains, and mountains are distant context, not the main subject.
- Never show a drone, aircraft, modern skyline, or impossible floating camera object.

## 6. Continuity rules

- Match Wu Zetian's life stage to makeup, hair, costume, status, and environment.
- Repeat the same black-gold coronation language across coronation shots unless the user requests a costume change.
- Keep architecture, palette, and daylight direction compatible across adjacent assets.
- Never generalize the beautified coronation age treatment to every historical figure.
- If historical precision is uncertain, use a plausible early Tang/Wu Zhou form and avoid unsupported exact claims.
